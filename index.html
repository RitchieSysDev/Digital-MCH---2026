Birth Plan: Preparing for Safe Delivery

Discuss with mother/couple

Father's Support for Mother & Child Health

1. During Pregnancy

  • • Ensure ANC starts within first 12 weeks & completes 8+ contacts
  • • Accompany to health visits, help with chores
  • • Support eating from 5+ of 10 food groups daily
  • • Get tested together for HIV, Hepatitis B, Syphilis
  • • If HIV+, help stay on treatment for viral suppression
  • • Develop birth plan together

2. During Childbirth

  • • Arrange transport, money, emergency supplies
  • • Encourage and offer comfort during labor
  • • Support chosen birthing position
  • • Ensure basic needs met at home

3. After Childbirth

  • • Ensure exclusive breastfeeding for first 6 months
  • • Spend time holding and caring for baby
  • • Help with house chores
  • • Support mother to eat 2 extra small meals daily
  • • Accompany to postnatal appointments
  • • Choose family planning together within 48 hours

Interventions for HIV Positive Mothers and Their Infants

Viral Load (VL) Testing

Note: Pretreatment VL for newly identified; Baseline VL for known HIV+ mothers.

Early Infant Diagnosis (EID) of HIV Exposed Infant (HEI)

All HEIs should be tested as per the schedule below:

Type of Test Date of Sample Collection Results
Birth testing (1st DNA PCR)
2nd DNA PCR at 6-8 weeks (or first contact if after 8 weeks)
3rd DNA PCR at 6 months
4th DNA PCR at 12 months
Final antibody test at 18 months (if not breastfeeding) or 6 weeks after complete cessation of breastfeeding

Key: DNA PCR = Deoxy Ribo Nucleic Acid Polymerase Chain Reaction; DBS = Dried blood spot. If any test positive, collect confirmatory DNA PCR and baseline VL, start ARV.

Treatment of Infected Mothers and Management of Exposed Infant

Treatment of Positive Mother

HIV
1st line: TDF+3TC+DTG
If renal insufficiency/osteoporosis: TAF+3TC+DTG
Treatment is lifelong
Syphilis
Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM stat
Or Ceftriaxone IM 1g OD for 10-14 days (penicillin allergy)
Hepatitis B
Start TAF/TDF+FTC or TAF/TDF+3TC (2nd trimester until delivery or completion of infant HBV vaccination)

Management of Exposed Infant

High Risk HIV Acquisition
ABC/3TC/DTG for 6 weeks* then extended NVP prophylaxis until 6 weeks after complete cessation of breastfeeding
Low Risk HIV Acquisition
Initiate NVP prophylaxis until 6 weeks after complete cessation of breastfeeding
For All HEIs
CTX Prophylaxis (from 6 weeks): CTX syrup 2.5ml OD
IPT syrup - Given as per child's eligibility
Hepatitis B Exposed
HBV birth dose 0.5ml within 24hrs + HBIG 0.5ml IM within 24hrs
All exposed infants should be breastfed exclusively for 6 months. Continue taking baby for immunization, Vitamin A/Micronutrients/deworming.

Feeding Sick Children
🤒

During Illness

  • ✓ Encourage the child to drink and eat - with lots of patience
  • ✓ Feed small amounts frequently
  • ✓ Give foods that the child likes
  • ✓ Give a variety of nutrient-rich foods
  • ✓ Continue to breastfeed – often ill children breastfeed more frequently
💪

During Recovery

  • ✓ Give extra breastfeeds
  • ✓ Feed an extra meal
  • ✓ Give extra amount of food
  • ✓ Use extra rich foods
  • ✓ Feed with extra patience and love
⚠️
Important Notes:
  • Introduce one type of food at a time
  • Do not mix more than 2 types of cereals
  • Give your child protein foods from 6 months
  • Use a separate plate/bowl to feed your baby
  • Observe hygiene! Wash hands at critical times
  • Keep cooking surfaces, utensils, play items and areas clean
  • Take your child to the clinic every month until he/she is 5 years old

When to Return Immediately to the Health Facility

🤒 Bring ANY Sick Child If:

  • ❌ Not able to drink or breastfeed
  • ❌ Becomes sicker
  • ❌ Develops fever

🫁 Bring Child with Cough If:

  • ❌ Fast breathing
  • ❌ Difficult breathing
  • ❌ Drinking poorly

👶 Bring Young Infant If:

  • ❌ Feels unusually cold/hot
  • ❌ Palms and soles appear yellow
  • ❌ Breast feeding poorly
  • ❌ Any of the above signs

💩 Bring Child with Diarrhoea If:

  • ❌ Blood in stool
  • ❌ Drinking poorly
💧

Fluids Recommendations for a Sick Child

💧
Giving more fluids can be life saving!

💧 For Children NOT on Exclusive Breastfeeding

Give extra fluids as much as the child will take:

  • • ORS and zinc solution
  • • Soup, porridge, yoghurt drink
  • • Clean and safe water
  • • Breastfeed more frequently and longer
  • • Continue giving extra fluids until diarrhoea stops

🍼 For Babies on Exclusive Breastfeeding

  • • Breastfeed more frequently and longer at each breastfeed
  • • Give ORS and Zinc solutions
  • If the child vomits: Wait for 10 minutes then give small frequent sips

✅ For ANY Sick Child

  • • Breastfeed frequently and for longer at each feed
  • • Increase fluid intake. Give soup, porridge, yoghurt drinks or clean and safe water, if on complementary feeding
