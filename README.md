# Digital-MCH---2026
Digital-MCH-®-2026 is a digital platform adopted and developed from the MoH 216: Mother Child Health Handbook, 2025 Edition to promote digitization of MCH services in Kenya.

TO THE MOTHER AND FATHER
Congratulations on this pregnancy! The Ministry of Health would like to celebrate with you and presents this Digital-MCH-®-2026 platform to you. Please read it well together with family members and understand the contents well. If you have any question, please ask a health worker or a Community Health Promoter (CHP) without any hesitation.
This Digital-MCH-®-2026 platform will be used during pregnancy, childbirth and after childbirth until the child is 5 years old. Please hand it over to the child when he/she is a young adult as a present with instructions to keep it safe. Your child will read its contents and understand his/her health history before birth until 5 years of age. The child will also realize your love, health workers’ and other service providers’ contribution towards protecting his/her life. We hope this MCH Handbook will help protect life of mother and child thus lead to; a healthy mother, child and family, and a healthy and prosperous Nation.

TO THE HEALTHCARE WORKER AND COMMUNITY HEALTH PROMOTER
This Digital-MCH-®-2026 platform is here to help MoH of Kenya drive the digitization of MoH 216: Mother Child Health Handbook. Please consider using this digital platform alongside the book to help you familiarize with its application in MCH clients to hone your skills in digital handling of MCH clients.
